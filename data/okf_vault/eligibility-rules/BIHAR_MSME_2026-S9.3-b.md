## Section 9.3(b) — Additional Incentive for Scaling Up

Enterprises that have already availed Scaling Up incentives and subsequently move to a lower category due to annual updation of Udyam Registration arising from reduction in turnover or written down value of plant and machinery shall not be eligible to claim Scaling Up incentives again upon re-graduation to a higher category.
