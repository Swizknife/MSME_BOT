## Section 9.3(a) — Additional Incentive for Scaling Up

Incentives sanctioned under the Scaling Up category shall be over and above the eligible Capital Subsidy or Special Capital Subsidy available for expansion, diversification, or modernization resulting in graduation to a higher enterprise category.
