## AMB-02 — undefined_term (advisory)

Section 7.4(ii) promises an additional 5% Capital Subsidy to 'backward districts as defined in the BIIPP Policy 2026', but Annexure I defines only Region A / Region B, never 'backward districts'.

**Clauses:** 7.4(ii), Annexure I  
**Pages:** 13, 25
