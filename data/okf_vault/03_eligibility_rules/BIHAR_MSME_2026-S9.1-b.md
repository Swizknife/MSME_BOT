## Section 9.1(b) — General Conditions for Capital Investment Incentives

The eligible incentive shall be disbursed in two equal instalments. The first instalment shall be released upon achievement of the prescribed project implementation milestones, while the second instalment shall be released after the unit achieves at least 50% of its commercial production capacity.
