## Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS)

TReDS is "an institutional mechanism to help MSMEs convert trade receivables
into liquid funds," financing "of trade receivables of MSMEs from corporate
and other buyers, including Government Departments and Public Sector
Undertakings (PSUs), through multiple financiers." Transactions processed
under TReDS are **without recourse** to the MSME seller.

Participants: MSME sellers; corporate/government/PSU buyers; financiers
("banks, NBFC Factors and other financial institutions as permitted by the
Reserve Bank of India"); and sellers'/buyers' bankers, who may get
read access to the discounted-invoice portfolio.

**No eligibility threshold is stated for MSME sellers or buyers themselves**
— only KYC documentation and master-agreement onboarding are required. The
₹25 crore paid-up capital requirement (see [[TREDS-OPERATOR-CAPITAL]]) applies
to the companies that OPERATE a TReDS platform, not to MSMEs using one.

Regulatory basis: "These Guidelines are issued by Reserve Bank of India
under Section 10(2) read with Section 18 of Payment & Settlement Systems
Act, 2007 (Act 51 of 2007)." TReDS is "an authorised payment system under
the PSS Act 2007."
